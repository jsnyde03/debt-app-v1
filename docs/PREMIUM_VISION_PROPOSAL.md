# Premium Vision Proposal

## Philosophy

The premium experience should no longer focus on **showing more information**. AI-generated text and "smart insights" are no longer enough to justify a subscription. Instead, premium should focus on **saving time, reducing effort, and actively managing a user's financial life**.

The free app should be incredibly capable on its own. Premium should feel like hiring an intelligent financial assistant.

---

# Pillar 1 — Automation

## Autopilot Payday

Instead of simply recommending what to pay, the app creates a complete paycheck execution plan.

Example:

✓ Pay Rent

✓ Pay Capital One

✓ Reserve Living Expenses

✓ Extra $150 to Discover

One tap executes the entire plan and marks everything complete.

---

## Smart Carry Forward

If a payment is skipped or only partially completed, automatically carry it into the next paycheck while recalculating the entire payoff strategy.

No manual adjustments required.

---

## Bill Drift Detection

Detect recurring bills that have quietly increased.

Example:

Internet

May: $89

June: $89

July: $107

Alert:

"Your internet bill increased by $18/month."

---

## Subscription Watch

Automatically identify recurring subscriptions.

Detect:

- Duplicate services
- Forgotten subscriptions
- Price increases
- Rarely used subscriptions

The app becomes something users check because it actively manages their finances—not because they have to.

Imagine opening the app and seeing:

Good morning.

✓ Payday tomorrow.

✓ Cell phone bill due Friday.

✓ You're $125 ahead this cycle.

✓ You'll reach 10% debt paid next paycheck.

⚠ Car insurance renews in 18 days.

✓ No action required today.

The app should evolve from a debt calculator into a financial operating system.

---

# Guiding Principle

A simple rule should guide every future premium feature:

> **If removing the feature only removes information, it probably isn't premium enough.**
>
> **If removing the feature forces the user to do significantly more work themselves, it is likely a strong premium feature.**

Premium should automate work, simplify decisions, and actively improve a user's financial life—not simply display more data.

# v1.1 Rejection Fix — Guideline 3.1.2(c) Subscription Disclosure

_Two related issues from the same guideline. Both are fixed in code; this doc covers what to paste where in App Store Connect for resubmission._

## What was wrong

1. **Billed amount wasn't the most prominent pricing element.** The upgrade screen showed feature names and a generic "Upgrade to Premium" button with no price anywhere on screen.
2. **Missing required subscription info in-app and in metadata.** Apple requires, inside the app's purchase flow: subscription title, length, price, and functional links to Privacy Policy + Terms of Use. The app description also needs a Terms of Use link if using Apple's Standard EULA.

## What was fixed in code

- `lib/subscription/revenueCat.ts` — new `getPremiumPackageInfo()` fetches the real, localized price/title/period from RevenueCat (not a hardcoded string).
- `components/UpgradeSection.tsx` — added a large, high-contrast price block (`$X.XX/month`, 2.5rem, accent color) positioned directly below the headline — now the single largest, boldest element on the screen, clearly more prominent than the feature list or any other text. Subtext directly under it states the subscription title and length ("Premium Monthly · billed every month, auto-renews until cancelled"). Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links were already present on this screen; both remain.
- The "Upgrade to Premium" button now also echoes the price (`Upgrade to Premium — $4.99/month`) for redundant clarity, sized smaller than the main price callout so it doesn't compete with it.
- `docs/release-notes/app-store-listing.md` — added a "Subscription Details" paragraph to the App Description with title, price, billing terms, and both required links (Terms of Use + Privacy Policy), since Apple requires this in the App Description when using the Standard EULA.

## What you need to do in App Store Connect

1. **Paste the updated App Description** (see `app-store-listing.md`'s Description section — the new "Subscription Details" paragraph is what Apple is specifically checking for) into the app's description field.
2. **Confirm the Privacy Policy URL field** in App Store Connect's app metadata (separate from the description) is still set to `https://github.com/jsnyde03/debt-planner-stie/blob/main/privacy.html`.
3. **Add this to the Notes field of the App Review Information section** (Apple explicitly asked for this for future submissions):

> This app uses Apple's Standard Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
> Privacy Policy: https://github.com/jsnyde03/debt-planner-stie/blob/main/privacy.html
> Auto-renewable subscription: Premium Monthly, $4.99/month (USD, may vary by region), billed monthly, auto-renews until cancelled. Title, price, length, and both links above are displayed directly in the in-app purchase screen (Payoff tab → "View Premium" → upgrade screen).

4. **Reply to the rejection message in Resolution Center with a screen recording** showing: opening the upgrade screen, the price displayed prominently, and tapping both the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links to confirm they're functional. Apple explicitly asked for this confirmation before they'll re-review.

## Reviewer walkthrough (for the screen recording / reply)

1. Launch the app, enter any paycheck amount, tap "Calculate plan."
2. Tap the "Payoff" tab — with no debts entered, tap "View Premium."
3. The upgrade screen shows: subscription title ("Premium Monthly"), the price in large bold text ("$4.99/month"), billing terms ("billed every month, auto-renews until cancelled"), and Privacy Policy / Terms of Use / Support links at the bottom.
4. Tap "Terms of Use" — confirms it opens Apple's Standard EULA page.
5. Tap "Privacy Policy" — confirms it opens the app's privacy policy page.

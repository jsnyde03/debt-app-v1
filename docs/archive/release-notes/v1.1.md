NEW FEATURES

Premium: Smart Forecasting (Subscription)
Paycheck Debt Planner now offers a premium subscription unlocking four analytical features:

  Smart Insights
  Adaptive guidance each pay cycle — whether to push extra payments, hold back to protect
  your cash cushion, or flag a near-payoff opportunity. Severity levels (stable, warning,
  risk) surface the most important signal at the top.

  Strategy Comparison
  Snowball vs. avalanche side-by-side using your actual balances and extra payment amount.
  Projected payoff dates, total interest, and a recommended strategy based on your numbers.

  What-If Simulation
  Enter any extra monthly payment and see the resulting debt-free date, months saved, and
  projected interest reduction before committing. Quick-pick chips (+$25, +$50, +$100,
  +$250) make it fast to explore scenarios. Shows exactly which debt gets the extra money
  and how much remains.

  3-Month Forecast
  Projects cash cushion, debt balance, and financial risk for the next three pay cycles.
  Each forecast month shows status (Stable / Tight / Pressure / Critical), a recommended
  action, risk drivers, and projected relief timing. A summary strip shows cushion trend
  and total debt reduction over the forecast window.

Swipe Gestures
  Every required action card and recommendation now supports swipe gestures. Swipe right
  to mark paid. Swipe left to undo a completed action or log a recommendation as paid
  outside your paycheck. The plan recalculates instantly.

Timeline View
  A new chronological timeline shows your complete pay cycle — paycheck received, bills
  due, debt minimums, autopay items, emergency fund contributions, and snowball payments —
  with a running safe-cash balance at every step. Items are labeled with type pills
  (Autopay, Recommended, Planned) so the full picture is clear at a glance.

Autopay Tracking
  Mark any bill or debt as autopay. Autopay items are flagged throughout the plan, required
  actions list, and timeline so you always know what hits automatically vs. what needs a
  manual payment.

Editable Recommendations
  Recommended emergency fund and extra debt payment amounts can now be adjusted to a
  custom amount before marking complete. Overrides persist for the full pay cycle.

Goals Section
  Track emergency funds and savings goals alongside debt payoff. The allocation engine
  funds emergency goals before snowball payments. Goals show a progress bar, percent
  funded, and remaining amount. Fully-funded goals receive a "Funded" badge.

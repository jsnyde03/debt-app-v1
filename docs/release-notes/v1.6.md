## App Store "What's New" copy

**Payday Autopilot — your whole paycheck, confirmed in one tap.**
On payday, Debt Planner surfaces everything this paycheck covers — your required bills and minimums *plus* your extra payments — and lets you confirm it all in a single tap. Paid a bill differently, or one didn't go through? Tap Adjust to reconcile just that item; the rest stays one-tap. Your progress stays accurate without any bookkeeping.

**Autopay, handled.**
Mark a bill or debt minimum as autopay and Debt Planner stops nagging you to check it off — it shows an "Autopay" status, assumes it ran once due, and never lets a paid-by-autopay bill pile up as falsely *overdue* next paycheck. If an autopay didn't go through, report it at payday — it turns into a clear **Overdue** item you can pay off right from your plan, then quietly goes back to autopay next cycle.

**See what your extra payments are worth.**
A new Interest-Saved card on the Payoff tab shows exactly how much interest your plan saves you — and how much sooner you'll be debt-free — versus making only minimum payments. Free, and updated every time you run your plan.

**"What do I pay THIS paycheck" front and center.**
The Plan hero now leads with your next action — the exact payment for this paycheck — with your debt-free date as reassurance underneath.

**More accurate payoff dates.**
Debt-free dates and interest-saved figures now reflect your true number of paychecks per month, so weekly and biweekly plans project correctly.

**Fixes & polish**
- The payday check-in is now fully touch-first: tap any recommended payment to mark it paid or skipped, tap an amount to edit it, and a one-tap "Mark all paid" in Adjust. Opening Adjust out of curiosity no longer changes anything unless you actually toggle a bill.
- Demo mode now follows your device's Light/Dark (Auto/System) setting instead of always opening in light mode.
- A start-next-cycle prompt appears after you log a payday, so your plan always rolls forward cleanly.
- Debts you pay off now clear completely and celebrate — no stray cents reappearing next cycle.
- Payments to a goal you'd already partly funded this cycle now capture correctly.
- Clearer messaging when you're debt-free or just getting started.

New in v1.6: Payday Autopilot (one-tap payday plan capture) and an Interest-Saved card on the Payoff tab.

HOW TO SEE PAYDAY AUTOPILOT:
The payday sheet appears when the device date reaches your next payday. To trigger it during review, open Plan Settings and set a paycheck whose next payday is today (or 1–3 days ago), tap Calculate, then relaunch — the "It's payday" sheet auto-opens.

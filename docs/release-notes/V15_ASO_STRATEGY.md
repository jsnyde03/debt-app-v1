_Authored 2026-07-02 for the v1.5 "Track Your Journey" release. This is the **strategy + research** half of the ASO package; the ASC-ready copy is in [app-store-listing.md](app-store-listing.md) and the shot plan is in [screenshot-brief.md](screenshot-brief.md). Based on a live 2026 US-iOS competitor + keyword teardown. **The app name stays "Paycheck Debt Planner"** — renaming a live app resets keyword history (high-risk, low-reward)._

## 1. The positioning wedge (validated + sharpened)

Live research validated the hypothesis but sharpened it: the market splits into three lanes, and **two of our four pillars are now contested**, so the moat is the *combination*, not any single pillar:

- **Debt-first calculators** (Debt Payoff Planner, Debt Snowball, Debt Payoff Box, DebtFree) own *snowball / avalanche / debt-free date* — but are **cycle-blind** (no "what to pay this paycheck").
- **Budget-first** (PayCheck Budget, EveryTwo) own *paycheck budgeting* — but have **no debt engine**.
- **Automation/fintech** (Qoins, Bright Money) are **bank-linked** — the opposite of our on-device/private story.
- **Journey/motivation** is *starting* to be contested: **"Debt Free – Pay Off your Debt"** now advertises a "Visual Journey Timeline." So "nobody owns motivation" is no longer strictly true — **v1.5 should move now** while the lane is still open.

**The open gap = the four-way intersection no single app occupies:**
1. paycheck-cycle "what to pay **this payday**" +
2. a real **snowball/avalanche** debt engine +
3. the **motivational journey** (streaks / milestones / history — v1.5) +
4. **100% on-device**, no bank login.

**Sharpest positioning (one sentence):**
> *The moment you get paid, it tells you exactly which bill and which debt to hit this paycheck — then celebrates every streak and milestone to debt-free, 100% private on your phone.*

**Tagline form:** **"Payday-by-payday debt payoff — private, no bank login."**

Lead the v1.5 creative with pillars **(1) "this payday, do this"** + **(3) the Track-Your-Journey streaks/milestones** — the pairing nobody else fields together.

## 2. Metadata recommendations (hand-counted)

**Only Name + Subtitle + Keyword field are search-indexed on iOS (160 chars total). The description and promo text are NOT indexed.** So every ranking keyword must live in those three fields; the description is pure conversion copy.

### Subtitle — CHANGE
- **Current (v1.2):** `Budget, Bills & Debt Payoff` — wastes an indexed slot repeating **Debt** (already in the name).
- **v1.5:** **`Snowball Budget & Bill Payoff`** — **29 chars.** Adds 4 new indexed words (*snowball, budget, bill, payoff*) and frees the name-combos "debt snowball," "paycheck budget," "bill payoff," "budget/payoff planner."

### Keyword field — CHANGE (99 chars)
```
avalanche,calculator,biweekly,milestone,streak,tracker,loan,credit,card,expense,income,savings,goal
```
- **avalanche, calculator** — high-intent debt terms the name doesn't cover ("debt avalanche," "debt/payoff calculator").
- **biweekly** — the pay-cycle wedge; no debt app targets it.
- **milestone, streak** — the v1.5 journey differentiators; near-unclaimed in debt search.
- **tracker** — broad combiner now that the subtitle uses "budget," not "tracker."
- **loan, credit, card** — "loan payoff"; credit+card auto-combines to "credit card."
- **expense, income, savings, goal** — retained core finance intent.
- **Dropped & why:** `snowball` (now in the subtitle — never duplicate), `debt free` (*debt* is in the name; standalone "free" risks a "free app" misread), `finance` + `money manager` (saturated heads; the phrase wastes chars), `cashflow` and plural `goals` (deprioritized; plurals auto-covered).

### Keyword-field hygiene (live-verified 2026 mechanics)
- Apple **auto-combines** words across Name+Subtitle+Keyword into phrases → single words beat pre-built phrases.
- **Never repeat** a Name/Subtitle word in the keyword field (no benefit, wasted chars).
- **Singular only** (plurals auto-covered); **no spaces after commas**.
- **Trademark — DO NOT USE (Guideline 2.3.7):** Dave Ramsey, EveryDollar, YNAB, Mint, Rocket Money, Truebill, Undebt.it, Qoins, Bright, or any competitor brand. Generic method names (snowball, avalanche) are safe.

## 3. Beyond the 160 chars — 2026 mechanics to exploit

- **Screenshot caption OCR is now indexed (2025+).** Caption text is effectively *bonus* keyword space. Use it to carry intent that didn't fit the 160 chars — especially **payday, debt-free, private, what-if, forecast** (see the caption lines in [screenshot-brief.md](screenshot-brief.md)).
- **Custom Product Pages (up to 70/app; keyword-assignable to organic search since mid-2025):** worth **two CPPs** — one leading with the *paycheck-budgeting* shots (for "paycheck budget" searchers) and one leading with the *debt-snowball* shots (for "debt payoff" searchers). Same binary, different lead creative + assigned terms.
- **In-App Events (name + short description are indexed):** a **"Track Your Journey"** v1.5 launch event can carry `milestone` / `streak` intent into search and the Today tab.

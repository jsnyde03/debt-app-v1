Kills the self-contradiction (card "covered" while its own lookahead says "tight") and the false alarms that worsen as the floor rises.

## Cluster 2 — Voice & the anxious edges

- **D2.1 · The voice → active PLAN-SHAPING, not custody (Jason, 2026-07-23).** The Guardian owns the DECISION it made — *"I've set this paycheck to keep $200 as your cushion and put the spare to work — $400 to your emergency fund, $60 to debt. Mark them when you're ready."* Active, decisive, real agency (it shaped the plan) = the "acts, not narrates" identity. But NEVER custody voice (*"I'm holding / I sent / I moved"*) — nothing moves until the user marks paid. Completed-action words (held, paid, moved) reserved for actual executions (confirmed payoff, verified balance).

- **D2.2 · Guard false-REASSURANCE, not just false-alarm (Jason, 2026-07-23).** The clear state is an OBSERVATION, not a guarantee: *"Looks clear this paycheck"* (not "You're safe / covered"), and any deploy-the-spare-to-debt nudge carries a light hedge (*"…if nothing unexpected's coming up"*). Symmetric with the alarm-side caution — "covered" is never a promise the app can't keep (covered → deploy cash → surprise expense → overdraft is the more harmful error for a debt app).

- **D2.3 · Free taste ALWAYS helps — never alarm-then-upsell (Jason, 2026-07-23).** Free at-risk = the honest read + a genuine **universal** safe-move anyone can act on (*"cover your essentials first, hold off on extra debt payoff this cycle"*) + THEN the value-led invitation (*"Premium maps your way back over the next few paychecks"*). The personalized multi-cycle **recovery plan** (specific crunch pre-funding, exact set-asides) stays premium; generic sound guidance is never withheld from a stressed free user. Principle holds across states — at-risk non-negotiable; clear/tight keep a light helpful note for consistency ([[feedback_premium_gating_value_led]]).

- **D2.4 · One canonical money vocabulary; "cushion" reserved (Jason, 2026-07-23).** One set of bucket names across all of Today (Cushion · Emergency fund · Debt · Goals · Leftover, per D1.2); **"cushion" means EXACTLY the liquid, floor-protected, this-cycle safety** — nowhere else. The hero's Required/Everyday/Free breakdown reconciles to the same vocabulary so no two "cushion-ish" numbers ever collide. (Word "cushion" kept; may revisit at spec-writing if a sharper term surfaces.)

## Cluster 3 — Variable / irregular income

- **D3.1 · Core principle → PLAN FOR THE VALLEY (Jason, 2026-07-23).** For variable income (hourly / tips / commission / overtime — mainstream, not gig-only), build the cushion + the plan on the **conservative (lean) end** of income; treat anything above as a **bonus** that flows into the waterfall (EF → debt → goals). Honest AND genuinely sound advice (plan for valleys, celebrate peaks); reinforces the safety ethos and flips income uncertainty into a positive. A bad paycheck never breaks the plan because the plan never assumed a good one. Framing: *"Your income varies, so I plan on a lean paycheck (~$X) — a slow one won't sink you, and anything more is bonus progress."*

- **D3.2 · Variable-income capture → (2) lean + typical (Jason, 2026-07-23).** A **"my income varies" toggle** (fixed-income users unaffected) → **lowest reliable paycheck** (REQUIRED — the plan anchor; the valley principle runs on it) + **typical paycheck** (optional — context for "you usually make ~$Y; anything above your lean line is bonus"). **Guardrail:** the lean number is a *floor you can count on*, NOT a guess — the prompt coaches this ("the amount you can reliably count on even on a slow stretch"), else the whole safety benefit evaporates. Not a 3-point range (more friction than the valley principle needs).

- **D3.3 · Learn the income range from real paychecks → suggest-not-silent, IN v1.7 (Jason, 2026-07-23).** Record each **actual** paycheck at payday (cheap; also feeds the D5 calibration loop). Once there's enough history, **suggest** a refinement (*"your last 6 ran $1,850–$2,400; set your lean to $1,850?"*) — user confirms, NEVER silent. Enables trend detection (*"income slipped ~15% over three months"*). **Both the data capture AND the learning/suggestion layer are v1.7 scope (Jason: in v1.7 regardless, not a fast-follow).** The un-chattable "learns your money" premium story.

## Cluster 4 — Debt-free / graduation

Same guardian, new chapter — no jarring app-swap. **Fix:** `selectPaydayGuardian` must not return null at debt-free.

## Cluster 5 — Cash-flow-brain mechanics (2.4.7)

- **D5.1 · Recovery math → running PREFIX-SUM + per-cycle NET (confirmed, Jason 2026-07-23).** Timing-vs-structural = can each deficit be covered from surplus in EARLIER cycles (`cumulative_surplus(1..k−1) ≥ cumulative_deficit(1..k)`), not a whole-horizon sum (money can't flow backward); the first cycle the prefix test fails = the timing/structural boundary. The plan consumes a clean per-cycle **net** (income − required − living), NOT `endingBalance`.
- **D5.2 · Drill-down = a ROUTE = the elevated Progress cushion view (confirmed, Jason 2026-07-23).** Reached FROM the Guardian; one cash-flow destination, two entry points — not a third parallel cushion surface on Today.
- **D5.3 · EF rung (audit D6) → gate + choice-with-why + no blame (Jason, 2026-07-23).** Gate the starter-EF recommendation behind a one-tap *"already have savings set aside elsewhere?"* (absence never assumed = need); offer EF-first-vs-APR-first as a **choice with a why** (a real finance disagreement, not a verdict); for structural deficits, describe the **gap** (*"~$180/cycle short over the next six"*) without naming/blaming a guessed "flexible" cost.

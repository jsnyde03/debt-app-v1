# Premium Elevation — Third Audit (round 3, against spec v2), synthesis (2026-07-23)

Five **rotated** angles on **spec v2** (which folded in every round-2 fix): a red-team (make it fail a real user), a fresh-eyes builder read, a competitor teardown, and correctness + trust **re-verifications** of the round-2 fixes. Each was asked to call **converged** (only polish left) or **structural**.

## Convergence verdict: **NOT converged — 4 of 5 angles structural.** But this is *progress*, not spinning.
Round 1 found broad bugs; round 2 found scope + the missing substrate; **round 3's findings collapse onto three themes** — and two of them are *specific and closable*. The loop is narrowing.
- **Red-team → structural.** Competitor → *converged on the wedge* (positioning fix only). Fresh-eyes → structural. Correctness → structural (4 blockers). Trust → structural.
- **Notably, round 3 caught two round-2 fixes that DON'T hold as I specced them** (F3 partition, F1 smoothing) and one round-2 fix that was itself *wrong* (F7 living-expense). That's the re-verification earning its keep.

## THEME A — the missing **confidence-governance layer** (red-team + trust *unify* — a NEW design layer)
v2 hardened the *honesty of the read* but never made either the **acting** or the **hedging** proportional to how confident/complete the *inputs* are. Two lenses hit the same hole from opposite sides:
- **Red-team (action side):** the boundary is *stated, not enforced.* The Guardian confidently presents "deploy the spare $400" on top of an **incomplete bill list**, an **unverified cold-start lean**, or **3-month-stale balances** — so the app can *induce* the overdraft it disclaimed. Disclaimers protect the app's credibility, not the user's checking account.
- **Trust (voice side):** the honest hedges are **locally correct but globally corrosive** — four hedges (stale-balance · still-learning · observation-not-guarantee · planned-on-lean) stack on one sentence, so the flagship reads as "an app that doesn't trust itself," contradicting the decisive "watches + acts" identity. And **2.1 (staleness hedge) contradicts 2.3 (always-current)** — auto-maintenance exists so users *don't* re-verify, so the hedge fires *most* cycles.
- **The unified fix (v3):** one **confidence policy** keyed on input-confidence (bill-completeness · lean-verification depth · estimate-staleness · income-modelability) that governs **both**: (1) **action** — dampen the deploy / hold a "discovery buffer" / a **hard staleness cutoff** beyond which the Guardian stops naming deploy amounts and the "covered → deploy" state entirely; (2) **voice** — a **hedge budget** (surface at most one hedge, chosen by the dominant uncertainty; the rest adjust confidence silently), and a **cold-start reframed as a *value* state** ("I'm watching from day one; track record after a few cycles") so the just-converted user isn't met with "still learning." This is genuinely new — neither the review nor round 2 conceived it.

## THEME B — the **engine-mechanics gap**: v2 named outcomes, not mechanisms (fresh-eyes + correctness)
v2 is a strong *design* doc but not a *buildable* engine spec — load-bearing mechanisms are "nouns without a mechanism," and several are specced **wrong**:
- **F3 · the partition fix DOESN'T fix the lie (structural honesty).** The engine emits "Keep cash buffer" *and* "Leftover cash" under the **same `leftover` category**; `selectLiquidCushion` sums the whole category → "holding **$700** as your cushion" when only $200 is the floor. **The round-1/C4 lie survives**, and the reconciliation test *passes* while the number is wrong (false confidence). **Fix: split the floor-buffer into its own category; cushion = floor-buffer only; "true leftover" must actually exist.**
- **F1 · floor-aware smoothing is specced WRONG (over-conservative → invents crunches).** `max(0, net−floor)` reserves `k×floor` across k cycles when only **1×floor** of real cash is ever held. Net +300/+300/−250 (floor 200) never dips below floor in reality, but the formula falsely declares a structural gap → a false-alarm, the HARD-forbidden class. **Fix: model a running balance with carryover (`bal_k = bal_{k−1}+net_k`; boundary = first k where `bal_k < floor`); reserve the floor once.**
- **F2 · the waterfall re-arch breaks the reconciliation test + one rung is architecturally impossible.** New rungs (pre-fund, starter-vs-fuller-EF) have **no categories** → pre-fund lands as `leftover` → overstates cushion or fails the test. And **"pre-fund crunches" can't live inside the single-cycle `allocatePaycheck`** — it's inherently multi-cycle; it must feed the allocator as an *input*, not sit as a rung. "high-APR debt" also contradicts snowball mode. **Fix: enumerate new categories + update all 4 category-summing selectors + reconciliation; move pre-funding to the multi-cycle layer.**
- **F4 · hysteresis + one-metric only reached the card; the forecast/lookahead stays stateless, `endingBalance`-based, fixed-threshold** — and there are **three** state-producers in code (card · forecast · `selectPlanSummary`), not the two v2 names. So the card ("clear") and its own lookahead ("tight") still disagree — the exact contradiction v2 claims to kill. **Fix: put per-cycle `discretionary` on `TimelineCycle`, one shared `computeState(discretionary, floor, priorBand)`, migrate all three call sites, thread the prior band through the projection loop.**
- **F5 · prediction-stamp has no re-stamp policy** → a mid-cycle edit means the scorecard grades a stale cycle-start prediction → corrupts the calibration loop. **Fix: on a material change, re-stamp + flag the cycle "disturbed"; exclude disturbed cycles from the N≥4 count.**
- **F6 · "robust low percentile" ≈ the raw min at the N=4–6 it ships behind** (the same failure it was meant to prevent). **Fix: low-N shrinkage toward `typical` until N~12–15, then the percentile.**
- **F8 · un-nulling at debt-free hits a branch that labels EF/goals/wealth as "cushion."** **Fix: a debt-free-aware branch (deploy target = EF/goals/wealth).**
- **Cross-cutting · "one canonical debt-free date" has THREE producers** (state-threaded lean forecast · `selectDebtFreeDate`'s separate `projectDebtPayoff` · aspirational typical); the band needs **two** threaded projections, v2 specs one. **Fix: reconcile to one engine + two runs (lean, typical).**
- **REVERSE a round-2 item (F7): do NOT "fix living-expense cadence."** Capture is **already per-paycheck by design** (the UI says "set aside each paycheck"); a naive "cadence-defaults-monthly + prorate" migration would **divide every existing user's amount by 2–4×** → a false-clear for the whole install base. Round 2 misdiagnosed this; drop it.

## THEME C — positioning + honesty micro-fixes (competitor + trust)
- **Retire two of the four moat claims (competitor).** "Un-chattable" and "watches so you don't have to" are the weakest / fastest-decaying (ChatGPT now has memory + scheduled tasks; Rocket literally watches your real account). **Lead with the two that hold: engine-grounded honesty (can't fake your numbers) + automated payday-cadence debt-payoff focus (zero YNAB-style hand-budgeting).** The wedge is real and the lane is empty, but it's *positional + execution-gated*, not a technical barrier — the manual-entry/retention exposure is the deliberate privacy tradeoff.
- **Honesty micro-fixes (trust):** the bad-record copy must **own a false-*clear* miss** ("I've under-warned — I've tightened my read"), never spin it as "over-cautious"; the **notification banner must under-claim** ("time to check this paycheck," neutral — not "looks tight," which a reconcile-to-clear turns into cried-wolf on the lock screen); the **valley interest-cost `$X` must be rounded/qualitative** (it rides the not-yet-proven forecast); the **advice boundary needs a persistent "your call" affordance + a two-sided-vs-verdict classifier**; **verify the ADP-detection API exists** before shipping conditional "E2EE" wording (drop the word if it doesn't) and reconcile the stale capstone-doc E2EE line; for **fixed-income users the scorecard still "holds by construction"** (the bonus-side fix is variable-income only) → score obligation-timing/headroom for them.

## The distinction that decides what happens next (my read)
Round 3's findings split cleanly:
- **Design-level (belongs in v3, decide before building):** Theme A (the confidence policy — new), F1 (corrected smoothing model), F3 (corrected partition), F7 (reverse the living-expense fix), the F4 three-producer unification, Theme C (positioning + honesty micro-fixes). These are *decisions*, and a couple are *corrections to wrong v2 fixes* — they must be right on paper.
- **Build-time mechanics (better nailed in the structure-first build than in another spec round):** the exact `band` value, the percentile method + shrinkage constants, the precise loop order, the category names, the prediction-stamp trigger event. A spec will never be as precise as **code + reconciliation tests**, and these are *exactly* what test-first building (2.4.D structure-first, the plan's anti-debt move) catches. Auditing a prose spec for "does it contain every formula" can loop forever with diminishing return.

## Recommendation
1. **Write v3** folding in the *design-level* findings (Theme A confidence policy · F1/F3/F7 corrections · F4 unification · Theme C positioning + honesty micro-fixes). Two of these fix *wrong* v2 fixes, so v3 is a genuine correctness step, not a polish pass.
2. **Then a focused round-4** on the *new* design surface only — the **confidence-governance layer** (Theme A) — since it's load-bearing and unaudited; not a fifth full re-tread.
3. **Recognize the inflection:** the Theme-B mechanics are converging into "spec detail," which **test-first building resolves better than more audit rounds.** So after v3 + the focused round-4 on Theme A, the highest-value next move is likely to **start the structure-first build (2.4.D)** and let reconciliation tests be the ongoing ratchet for the mechanics — rather than chase an ever-more-detailed prose spec. Jason owns the "how strict / when to build" call; this is the honest read on where the loop stops paying and building starts.
